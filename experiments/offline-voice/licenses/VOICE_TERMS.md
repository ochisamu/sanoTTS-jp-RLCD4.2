## English explanation

The generated voice must not be used to attack/criticize people (as defined by
the upstream character terms), solicit support/opposition for particular political,
religious or ideological positions, publish strong expressions without appropriate
audience zoning, or grant others permission to reuse it as source material.
Preserve upstream corpus terms for derived portions and pass those terms to
recipients when modifying or redistributing the software/model.
This is a convenience explanation, not a replacement for the original Japanese
terms or the upstream model license. Distribution of a finished work is different
from distributing reusable voice material. No unrestricted MIT grant covers the voice.

The face is original generic UI geometry, not a newly branded character voice or
Tsukuyomi-chan character artwork. No endorsement is implied. Before developing a
different character-specific voice product, consult the upstream provider's terms.
